The {{ term("credential") }} always leaves from the server's own address, never through the {{ term("rotator") }}. Caller requests carry it only for the endpoints the Endpoint policy sends with Roxy's account; Roxy's own {{ term("credential-check", "credential checks") }} (the probes listed on this page, which ask Roblox whether the cookie still works) use it too, about 50 to 60 calls a day. Its calls are paced by a small {{ term("bucket") }} with a share reserved for those checks, and a Roblox 429 gives it a {{ term("cooldown") }}.
