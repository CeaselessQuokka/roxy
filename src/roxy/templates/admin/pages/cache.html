When two callers ask the same question, Roxy asks Roblox once and gives both the same answer. That is the whole idea, and it is the only control that does not depend on who is asking: a Roblox game reaches Roxy from hundreds of server addresses, so per-address limits see a new caller each time, while Roblox sees one proxy asking the same thing again and again. Answering the repeats from the cache is what keeps Roblox from rate-limiting Roxy.
