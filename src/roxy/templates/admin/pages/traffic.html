Outcomes: served by Roblox means Roxy called Roblox and passed the answer on; served from the cache means Roblox never saw the request ({{ term("hit", "a cache hit") }} or a shared fetch, {{ term("coalescing") }}); refused means Roxy's own protection turned the caller away ({{ term("refusal") }}); failed means Roxy tried and could not answer (a Roblox error, a timeout, a {{ term("cooldown") }}).
