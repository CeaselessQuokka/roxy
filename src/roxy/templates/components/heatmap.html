What: `heatmap(id, title, rows, cols, values)` draws a grid of colored cells where darker means more, with a "Show numbers" switch that turns the same grid into a readable number table. Why: a picture of 168 cells is useless to a screen reader, and a second hidden table drifts from the picture. Here the picture IS a real with row and column headers and the number in every cell (visually hidden until "Show numbers" is on), so assistive technology always reads the exact data the eye sees (plan 14.9).
