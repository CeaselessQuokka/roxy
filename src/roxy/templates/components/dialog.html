Why A destructive action deserves one deliberate pause that says what will happen in plain words. How The form posts with htmx (`hx-post`, or `method="delete"` etc.), so the X-CSRF-Token header is added by static/js/htmx_setup.js. The server answers 2xx (the dialog closes; an `HX-Trigger` header can show a toast) or an error (the dialog stays open and shows the message). The confirm button stays disabled until the typed text matches, through the `typeConfirm` Alpine component (static/js/components.js).
